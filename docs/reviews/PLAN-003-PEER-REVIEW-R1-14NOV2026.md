# Independent peer review — PLAN-003

## 1. Verdict: `RED`

PLAN-003 has unresolved HIGH findings. Most importantly, it reopens a fixed owner constraint, incompletely closes C3, and permits the offline and six-stage gates to pass without proving the intended deployment and production path.

Review was read-only; no builds, installers, VMs, or tests were executed.
